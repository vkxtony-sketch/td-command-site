Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026

TD Command is a Windows desktop application and companion website for chess Tournament Directors, clubs, and organizers. This policy explains what information TD Command collects, why we collect it, how it is stored, and the choices you have.

TD Command is an independent software project. In this policy, “TD Command,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the project and its developer. “You” refers to the person using the application or website.

1. Information we collect

Account information from Google. TD Command uses Google sign-in. When you sign in, Google provides your name, email address, profile photo URL, and a Google account identifier. We use this to create your TD Command profile and to identify you when you return.

Workspace and organization information. When you use the app, we store the information you enter or create, including:

  • Workspace and organization names, usernames, descriptions, locations, and organization type.
  • Workspace memberships and roles, such as owner, admin, chief TD, TD, assistant, or viewer.
  • Invitations, including the invited email address, assigned role, creation time, expiry, and whether an invitation was accepted or revoked.
  • Audit records describing actions such as creating an organization, inviting a member, accepting an invitation, or revoking an invitation.

Communications. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

Local technical information. The desktop app writes diagnostic logs on your own computer. These logs are stored locally to help troubleshoot problems and are not automatically uploaded to us. The app also stores your encrypted sign-in session on your computer.

Website information. Our website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process standard technical information, such as IP addresses and browser details, as described in GitHub’s own privacy statement.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your sign-in session.
  • Create your profile and your personal workspace.
  • Create and operate organizations, memberships, roles, and invitations.
  • Enforce access controls and keep organization data separate.
  • Maintain audit records and protect the security of the service.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate with you about the app.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the app and the workspace features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the service, prevent abuse, and maintain audit records.
  • Consent: where you have given it, for example by choosing to sign in with Google.
  • Legal obligation: where we must retain or disclose information by law.

4. How information is stored and protected

Account, workspace, organization, membership, invitation, and audit data are stored in a Supabase project (Supabase Auth and PostgreSQL). Google provides the sign-in identity. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. These providers process information on our behalf or as independent providers under their own terms.

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including:

  • HTTPS for data in transit.
  • Supabase Auth with the PKCE flow for Google sign-in.
  • PostgreSQL Row Level Security so users can reach only the workspaces they belong to.
  • Controlled database functions for sensitive writes, such as invitations and memberships.
  • Windows DPAPI encryption for the sign-in session stored on your PC.
  • Access controls that limit who can view organization data.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the app at your own risk.

Some providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

5. When we share information

We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. Supabase hosts authentication and the database; Google provides sign-in; GitHub Pages hosts this website. These providers process information to deliver their services.
  • Your organization. People who share a workspace with you can see the profile and workspace information that the app makes available to members, including your name, email, role, and relevant workspace activity.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the service.
  • Business changes. If the project is reorganized or transferred, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information.

6. Data retention

We keep account, profile, workspace, and organization information while your account is active and for as long as needed to operate the service. Invitations expire after seven days, although records of invitations and related audit events may be kept while the workspace exists.

Local diagnostic logs remain on your computer until you clear them or uninstall the app. Website hosting logs are retained according to GitHub’s practices.

When you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymize the information we control, except where we must keep it for legal, security, or audit purposes.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Receive a portable copy of information you provided.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, email vkxtony@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. You can also sign out of the app at any time and remove the local session by signing out or uninstalling the app.

8. Children’s privacy

TD Command is intended for adults and organizations that run chess events. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where a higher age applies). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Tournament organizers may eventually enter information about players, including minors. Organizers are responsible for having a lawful basis to process that information and for obtaining parental or guardian consent where required.

9. Cookies and tracking

The desktop app does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. It stores your encrypted sign-in session and limited local preferences on your computer. This website does not set cookies for advertising or analytics. GitHub Pages may process standard request logs as part of hosting.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app develops. When we do, we will change the effective date above. If a change is material, we will provide notice in the app or by email where appropriate. Continuing to use TD Command after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email vkxtony@gmail.com.
