WHAT IS INCLUDED

Built for the director’s desk

Personal workspace

A private workspace is created automatically the first time you sign in, so you can start organizing immediately.

Organizations

Create a club, academy, scholastic program, or independent practice and keep its work separate from your personal space.

Roles and access

Owners, admins, chief TDs, TDs, assistants, and viewers each get the access their job requires.

Invitations

Invite colleagues by email with one-use links that expire after seven days and are tied to the invited address.

Secure sign-in

Google sign-in opens in your default browser and returns to the app. Your session is encrypted on your PC with Windows DPAPI.

Desktop first

A native Windows window, a single portable executable, and no browser tabs to lose track of while you run an event.

One account. Multiple organizations.

Your Google account is your identity. Create or join as many organizations as you need, and keep each roster, invitation, and role separate. Ask for access to the private preview.
